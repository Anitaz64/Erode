# Erode
Erode - an open-source typeface by Anita Brémond
Erode
Erode is a serif typeface family comprising 2 styles (Regular and Display) and 406 glyphs, released in 2026. Its name evokes a damaged structure, worn down by chemical action.
Its serif structure serves as a skeleton for an organic proliferation: each style represents a different degree of contamination, from a discreet graft to a total takeover. The living reclaims its rights over the constructed.
The aesthetic draws from the trash and DIY world of the techno/rave scene, as well as from the imagery of contamination and symbiosis found in certain contemporary fiction, such as Parasyte by Hitoshi Iwaaki, 1988, and The Last of Us by Naughty Dog, 2013, where the host organism and the parasite ultimately become indistinguishable.
